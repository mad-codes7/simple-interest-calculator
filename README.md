# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple interest calculator calculates the interest earned or paid on a principal amount at a fixed annual interest rate over a specified time.

## Formula

```text
Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100
Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest
```

## Inputs

- Principal (P): The initial amount invested or borrowed.
- Rate (R): The annual interest rate, expressed as a percentage.
- Time (T): The duration in years. Convert months to years by dividing by 12.

## Outputs

- Simple interest earned or payable.
- Total amount after adding the interest to the principal.

## Example

For a principal of 10,000, an annual interest rate of 5%, and a duration of 2 years:

```text
Simple Interest = (10000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 1000
Total Amount = 10000 + 1000 = 11000
```

## Calculation Steps

1. Enter the principal amount.
2. Enter the annual interest rate as a percentage.
3. Enter the duration in years.
4. Multiply the principal, rate, and time, then divide by 100.
5. Add the calculated interest to the principal to obtain the total amount.

## Notes

Simple interest is calculated only on the original principal; it does not compound. Use non-negative numeric inputs. This repository documents the calculator and its calculation method.
